# Appearance and motion

Use the default [Ragdoll illustration](ragdoll.png) as the visual reference for new breeds. Its blue eyes, seal points, ivory mittens and plume tail are the distinguishing features of this coat. Match its softly realistic painted fur, natural facial proportions, diffuse lighting, front-facing seated pose and relative scale. Keep a transparent silhouette, separate animated forelegs and pupil-free irises for the gaze overlay. Review the assembled pet at desktop size. Bombay pairs a sleek black coat with copper eyes. Label each cat with its English breed name followed by its Traditional Chinese breed name in full-width parentheses. Its unpainted pupils, eye anchors, shoulder anchors, illustration bounds and matching painted foreleg belong to CatSkin. The renderer adds two moving pupils and a textured foreleg for each shoulder, bent through the elbow and wrist. All coats use the compact keyboard. The eyes follow the pointer and the right paw moves a matching mouse beside the compact keyboard. During typing, both paws tap and the pupils ease down toward the keyboard. Moving the pointer or pausing typing returns the gaze to the pointer. In the pickup pose the right paw holds the mouse and the left holds the selected keyboard. Horizontal drag travel drives a damped swing around the grip, limited to 20 degrees in each direction.

Buttermilk is an independent keyboard finish. KeyCap defines physical geometry and hardware codes. The board and printed legends face the cat. Popup glyphs face the viewer. The menu selects each style independently. Graphite supplies a dark deck with slate accents. A differently proportioned cat needs calibrated anchors. Additional physical keyboard layouts need their own key map.

The display link supplies the frame clock. The exponential response uses elapsed time. At rest, the callback runs at a reduced cadence and only invalidates the view for motion or active glyphs.
